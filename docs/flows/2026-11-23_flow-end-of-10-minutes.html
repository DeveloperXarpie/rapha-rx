Flow: end of 10 minutes

What happens when Memory's 10 minutes of board time run out during a Station Master round, through the scorecard and the 10-minute summary, back to Home with Focus unlocked. The same path repeats for Focus and Planning; after Planning, Home offers Free Play.

Reference game: Station Master (train-yard, Memory) Phones are the 360 × 780 design canvas at 75%. Rows wrap on a narrow screen; read left to right.

A. The round that crosses 10 minutes

The check runs only when a level ends, so a level always finishes before the category completes. Passing 10:00 mid-round changes nothing on screen.
