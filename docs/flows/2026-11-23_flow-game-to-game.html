Flow: game to game (inside the 10 minutes)

What a resident sees from Home through Station Master and on to the next round or the next game, while the open category still has time left. Only board time counts toward the 10 minutes: the ticker runs while the board is on screen, the exit dialog is closed and the tab is visible.

Reference game: Station Master (train-yard, Memory) Phones are the 360 × 780 design canvas at 75%. Rows wrap on a narrow screen; read left to right.

A. First game of the day

Each day starts with only Memory open. The resident can tap either Memory game; here they pick Station Master.

B. Round end, inside the 10 minutes

The round ends on Station Master's own card. Its button reports the level to GameShell; with time still left in Memory, the next round starts at once on the same route. No scorecard appears between rounds.

C. Switching to another game

Minutes belong to the category, not the game. Leaving Station Master and playing Shopping List keeps adding to the same Memory 10 minutes.
